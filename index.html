ThePhoneSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This page describes how ThePhoneSearch handles information when using its eBay integration.

Information processed

The integration may process information provided by eBay that is necessary to manage product listings, inventory, orders and fulfilment. This can include listing data, stock information, order details and delivery information made available through eBay's APIs.

Purpose

Information is processed only for operating and maintaining ThePhoneSearch's eBay selling activity, including synchronising inventory, updating listings and handling order fulfilment.

Data sharing

Information is not sold. Data may be shared only where required to provide order fulfilment, comply with legal obligations, or operate the eBay integration.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect credentials and information handled by the integration. API credentials and access tokens are not intentionally published.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or where retention is required by law.

Your rights

Where applicable, individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information handled by ThePhoneSearch, subject to legal and contractual requirements.

Third-party services

The integration relies on eBay and may also communicate with authorised inventory or fulfilment providers. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact ThePhoneSearch through the contact details shown on its eBay seller profile.
